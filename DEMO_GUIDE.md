# GEOTHERM SHIELD: 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

Follow this step-by-step walkthrough during SIH judge presentations:

### Step 1: Strategic Cold-Desert Selection (Leh, Ladakh)
1. Launch dashboard at `http://localhost:3000`.
2. Notice the top KPI ribbon: **3,524m AMSL**, **94/100 Solar Score**, **91.5/100 Thermal Index**.
3. Under **Strategic SIH Test Sites**, click `Leh` or click directly on the high Himalayan plateau on the interactive MapLibre map.
4. Point out the layer toggles: **Solar Potential**, **Thermal Zone**, and **Elevation / Contour**.

### Step 2: 3D Digital Twin Parametric Orientation
1. Point to the **3D Parametric Digital Twin** on the right side.
2. Drag with the mouse to orbit around the shelter.
3. Highlight that the glazed apertures and rooftop solar array are dynamically oriented towards **True South (180° Azimuth)** at a **35° winter solar tilt**.

### Step 3: Dynamic 24-Hour Thermal RK4 Simulation
1. Scroll down to the **24-Hour Diurnal Thermal Simulation** chart.
2. Explain the contrast:
   - Exterior temperature (dashed gray curve) drops to **-16°C** at night.
   - Indoor temperature (solid teal curve) maintains a stable **15.6°C to 23.5°C** inside the green target comfort band.
3. Highlight the **GeoTherm Thermal Comfort Index (91.5/100)** and note that night freezing hazard is effectively mitigated.

### Step 4: Live "What-If?" Engineering Sandbox
1. Move the **PIR Insulation Thickness** slider from `12cm` to `5cm`.
2. Move the **Shelter Orientation** from `180°` to `135°`.
3. Click **Recalculate Physics**.
4. Show how the indoor temperature curve immediately drops, the critical heat loss period shifts, and the thermal comfort score penalizes the design.
5. Move the sliders back to `12cm` and `180°` to demonstrate restorative optimization.

### Step 5: Pareto Multi-Objective Co-Optimization
1. Point out the **Top 3 Recommended Pareto Designs**.
2. Explain why **Design Alpha** is selected as Rank 1: it balances thermal insulation, diurnal solar gain, and capital expenditure rather than optimizing either solar or insulation in isolation.

### Step 6: Government Subsidy & Financial Savings
1. Show the **Government Scheme Verification**:
   - `MNRE High Altitude Border & Himalayan Solar Cold-Desert Mission` provides up to ₹3,50,000 in capital support.
   - Replacing high-altitude diesel gensets saves **4,340 Liters of transported fuel annually** (~₹4,55,700/yr savings).
   - Simple payback period: **2.4 Years**.

### Step 7: Export Official Technical Report
1. Click the top-right button: **Export Report (PDF)**.
2. The browser immediately downloads `geotherm_shield_engineering_report.pdf` generated by Python ReportLab, complete with climate tables, simulation parameters, and official disclaimers.
