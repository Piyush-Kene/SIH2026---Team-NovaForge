# GEOTHERM SHIELD: Data Sources & Provenance

## 1. Meteorological & Microclimate Data
- **Primary Live Source**: [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) Forecast & Historical Reanalysis APIs (non-commercial open access).
- **Secondary Baseline / Fallback Source**: India Meteorological Department (IMD) high-altitude montane climatic statistics cross-correlated with NASA POWER (Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources) 22-year satellite solar climatology.

## 2. Thermophysical Envelope Materials
- **Standard Reference**: ASHRAE Handbook of Fundamentals & IS 3792 (Guide for heat insulation of buildings).
- **Materials Database**:
  - Polyurethane / PIR Sandwich Panels: $k = 0.024\text{ W/m}\cdot\text{K}$, $\rho = 40\text{ kg/m}^3$
  - Aerated Autoclaved Concrete (AAC): $k = 0.16\text{ W/m}\cdot\text{K}$, $\rho = 550\text{ kg/m}^3$
  - Aerogel Vacuum Insulation: $k = 0.014\text{ W/m}\cdot\text{K}$, $\rho = 160\text{ kg/m}^3$
  - Stabilized Rammed Earth: $k = 0.85\text{ W/m}\cdot\text{K}$, $\rho = 1900\text{ kg/m}^3$
  - Rock Wool Insulation: $k = 0.038\text{ W/m}\cdot\text{K}$, $\rho = 60\text{ kg/m}^3$

## 3. Solar PV & Electrical Benchmark Costs
- **MNRE Benchmark Cost Notifications**: Benchmarked at ~₹48,000–₹58,000 per kWp for remote high-altitude bifacial crystalline silicon arrays with galvanized steel ballasts.
- **Emission Factor**: Central Electricity Authority (CEA) CO2 Baseline Database for the Indian Power Sector ($0.71\text{ kg CO}_2\text{/kWh}$).
